Scope
This release changes the public product name from NN6 Power Plan to Game Power
Plan Switcher, executable/build metadata, current documentation and screenshots.
NN6 authorship, GPL terms and historical notices remain. Compatibility data,
startup, IPC, mutex, recovery and diagnostic identities retain their NN6 names.
The separate GUI, daemon and guardian remain separate.
